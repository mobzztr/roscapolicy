Keep your savings group's records straight

ROSCA is a record-keeping ledger for rotating savings and credit groups (also known as chit funds, committees, kameti or tontines). Group admins set up the group, add members, and record each round's dues, payments, winners and payouts so everyone sees the same history.

ROSCA never holds or moves money. Members pay each other outside the app (cash, UPI, bank transfer). The app only records what happened.
