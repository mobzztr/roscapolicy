Privacy policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · App: ROSCA: Group Savings Ledger (Android package network.rosca.mobile)

This policy explains what the ROSCA app collects, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. Questions: anjalsalam@gmail.com.

ROSCA is a record-keeping ledger. It helps rotating savings groups (chit funds, committees) record dues, payments, winners and payouts. The app never holds, transfers or moves money, and it does not process card or bank payments.

Device contacts

If you are a group admin and choose Pick from contacts, the app asks for permission to read your contacts. Your address book is read on your device only to show you a list to pick from. Only the contacts you select (their name and the phone number you choose) are uploaded and saved as members of your group. Your full contact list is never uploaded, and the app never changes or deletes your contacts. You can refuse the permission and type numbers instead.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising IDs, no ads, and no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No precise or approximate location.
  • No push-notification tokens (the app does not send push notifications).
  • No card numbers, and no bank log-ins. We never move money.
  • Photos: the app does not currently upload photos. If a future version lets you attach a payment screenshot, it will be optional, stored privately for your group's admin and payout recipients, and this policy will be updated first.

Technical data

Like any internet service, our providers receive your IP address and basic request data (time, app version, platform) when the app talks to them. The app's update service (Expo) receives the platform, app version and an anonymous installation ID when it checks for app updates.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the app: sign-in, showing your groups, recording rounds and payments.
  • To keep the group ledger accurate and auditable for all members.
  • To secure the service and prevent abuse (for example, sign-in rate limits).

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or build marketing profiles.

3. Who can see your information

  • Members of your groups can see your display name and the group's records (rounds, dues, payment status, winners, payouts). Group admins can see the phone numbers of members in their groups.
  • Service providers that process data for us, under their own security commitments:
    • Supabase (database, authentication and file storage). Data is stored in Supabase's Mumbai, India (ap-south-1) region.
    • An SMS provider used by Supabase Auth to deliver your one-time sign-in code. It receives your phone number and the code.
    • Expo / EAS (app update delivery), which receives the technical data described above.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

4. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS. Access to records is enforced on the server with row-level security, so you can only read groups you belong to and only your own payout details. Ledger entries are append-only to prevent silent changes.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can request deletion in the app (Profile > Delete account) or by emailing anjalsalam@gmail.com from, or with, your registered phone number. We may verify the request via that number.

  • Deleted within 30 days: your sign-in account, your profile display name and your private payout details.
  • Kept: group ledger records – your member entry (name and phone number as entered by the group admin), slots, dues, payments, winners, payouts and the audit log – because other members rely on them as a shared record, and for fraud prevention and dispute evidence. They are kept for as long as the group exists. Groups you administered stay visible to their members, unchanged, but can no longer be managed. If you sign up again with the same number, the kept records reconnect to your new account.

Details: account deletion page.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your display name and payout details on the Profile screen.
  • Deny or withdraw the contacts permission in Android Settings at any time.
  • Request a copy, correction or deletion of your data: delete your account or email anjalsalam@gmail.com.

Depending on where you live (for example, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the GDPR), you may have further rights, including to complain to a data-protection authority.

7. Children

ROSCA is intended for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. Material changes will be shown in the app.
