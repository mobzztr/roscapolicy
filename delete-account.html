Delete your account – ROSCA: Group Savings Ledger

App: ROSCA: Group Savings Ledger · Android package network.rosca.mobile · Effective 19 October 2026

This page explains how to ask us to delete your ROSCA: Group Savings Ledger account, what we delete, and what we keep and why.

How to request deletion

  • In the app: open Profile > Delete account. This opens this page; then send the email below.
  • By email: write to anjalsalam@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my ROSCA account” and include the phone number you sign in with.

Identity check

To protect your account, we may verify the request using your registered phone number (for example, by calling or messaging that number, or asking you to confirm from it) before we act on it.

What we delete (within 30 days)

  • Your sign-in account: you will no longer be able to sign in, and your sessions are ended.
  • Your profile display name.
  • Your private payout details (payout type and details such as a UPI ID or bank account).

What we keep, and why

ROSCA is a shared ledger. Other members of your groups rely on its records to know who paid, who won and who was paid out. We therefore keep the group ledger records:

  • your member entry in each group: the name and phone number as entered by the group admin;
  • slots, dues, payments, winners and payouts;
  • the group's audit log of these actions.

We keep them because other members rely on them as a shared record, and for fraud prevention and as evidence if there is a dispute. They are kept for as long as the group exists.

  • Groups you were an admin of stay visible to their members, unchanged, but can no longer be managed.
  • If you later sign up again with the same phone number, the kept group records reconnect to your new account.
ROSCA never holds or moves money, so there is no balance to withdraw before deleting your account.
